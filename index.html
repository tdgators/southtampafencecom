Small Lots, Historic Streets, Salt Air

South Tampa asks more of a fence than most neighborhoods. These are the things we plan around.

Historic Districts

Homes in local historic districts, like Hyde Park, may need historic preservation review for a new fence. We check your address before we design.

Compact Lots & Alleys

With homes close together and alleys behind them, privacy fencing has to work hard without overwhelming the house.

Near the Bay

Salt air is hard on bare steel, so we favor aluminum and vinyl for homes close to the water.

Front-Yard Rules

City rules generally keep front-yard fences lower than backyard fences. We design within them.
